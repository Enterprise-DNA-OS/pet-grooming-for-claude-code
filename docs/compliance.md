# Care records and rule sources

Checked 2026-10-03. This is a record review for a grooming business, not certification, veterinary advice or a complete legal audit. Operators must act on an animal's condition immediately. A missing record is a review flag, not proof that care was or was not given. The default reference jurisdiction is New Zealand. Australian operators must replace this rule set with their state or territory requirements before relying on it.

## New Zealand temporary housing

MPI's [Code of Welfare: Temporary Housing of Companion Animals](https://www.mpi.govt.nz/animals/animal-welfare/codes/all-animal-welfare-codes/code-of-welfare-temporary-housing-of-companion-animals) includes grooming establishments in its scope. [The code document](https://www.mpi.govt.nz/dmsdocument/30795/direct) is the source for the following checks.

- NZ-TH4: minimum standard 4 covers admission assessment and signed permission for emergency veterinary treatment when the owner cannot be contacted. The review flags missing admission observations for checked-in or completed appointments and missing consent references. A reference records the location of a signed form. It does not create consent.
- NZ-TH5: minimum standard 5 requires health observations at least twice daily and prompt attention for serious illness, distress or injury. Urgent unresolved incidents without a recorded response are flagged. The four-hour reminder during a checked-in stay is our conservative workflow reminder, not the statutory interval. This tool does not prove twice-daily care or monitor animals overnight. A groom card records who observed what and when.

No software substitutes for a trained person examining an animal or calling a vet. This base does not manage boarding, medication, quarantine, veterinary treatment or overnight stays. Vaccination and disease-control requirements depend on the facility, species and relevant code. Have the operator and their vet set the accepted evidence and exceptions.

## House rules

HOUSE-VACCINE flags pets with no unexpired evidence record. An unexpired record of any vaccination is only a starting check, not proof that every required vaccine is covered. Record the vaccine name and expiry supplied by the vet. Do not calculate medical schedules.

HOUSE-CONTACT flags missing emergency contact details. The default grooming cycle is 42 days, configurable per pet. It is a scheduling policy, not animal health guidance. Completion requires admission and handover observations. Records can show a missed appointment or overdue rebooking without concluding neglect.

## Privacy and access

The [New Zealand Privacy Commissioner's privacy principles](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/) cover collecting, storing, using and sharing personal information. Keep exports, care notes and drafts private. Before production, configure database roles, device encryption, backups, retention and access review. The local demo does not enforce staff accounts or row-level permissions. Consent for veterinary treatment is not marketing permission. Drafts never send.
